   169→   169→- reservations
   170→   170→- blockage state
   171→   171→- traffic direction
   172→   172→- congestion estimate
   173→   173→- relevant contextual memory
   174→   174→
   175→   175→A JEC does NOT:
   176→   176→

   178→   178→- own the global map
   179→   179→- compute global routes

   396→   396→8. PROSOCIAL ROUTING
   397→   397→==================================================
   398→   398→
   399→   399→Do not optimize every robot independently for shortest distance.
   400→   400→
   401→   401→Create a social-cost function.
   402→   402→
   403→   403→Initial model:
   404→   404→
   405→   405→route_cost =
   406→   406→w_distance * own_travel_time
   407→   407→+
   408→   408→w_wait * expected_wait
   409→   409→+
   410→   410→w_energy * estimated_energy
   411→   411→+
   412→   412→w_congestion * congestion_cost
   413→   413→+
   414→   414→w_externality * estimated_delay_imposed_on_local_fleet
   415→   415→+
   416→   416→w_risk * safety/risk_cost.
   417→   417→
   418→   418→The important term is:
   419→   419→
   420→   420→EXTERNALITY.
   421→   421→
   422→   422→Example:
   423→   423→
   424→   424→R1 saves 3 seconds by entering A4,
   425→   425→but causes three other robots a combined 16-second delay.
   426→   426→
   427→   427→The planner should be capable of selecting the slightly longer route.
   428→   428→
   429→   429→Limit externality calculation to the robot's local prediction horizon / conflict neighborhood so computation remains tractable.
   430→   430→
   431→   431→Make weights configurable from the dashboard/config file.
