   396→8. PROSOCIAL ROUTING
   397→==================================================
   398→
   399→Do not optimize every robot independently for shortest distance.
   400→
   401→Create a social-cost function.
   402→
   403→Initial model:
   404→
   405→route_cost =
   406→w_distance * own_travel_time
   407→+
   408→w_wait * expected_wait
   409→+
   410→w_energy * estimated_energy
   411→+
   412→w_congestion * congestion_cost
   413→+
   414→w_externality * estimated_delay_imposed_on_local_fleet
   415→+
   416→w_risk * safety/risk_cost.
   417→
   418→The important term is:
   419→
   420→EXTERNALITY.
   421→
   422→Example:
   423→
   424→R1 saves 3 seconds by entering A4,
   425→but causes three other robots a combined 16-second delay.
   426→
   427→The planner should be capable of selecting the slightly longer route.
   428→
   429→Limit externality calculation to the robot's local prediction horizon / conflict neighborhood so computation remains tractable.
   430→
   431→Make weights configurable from the dashboard/config file.
